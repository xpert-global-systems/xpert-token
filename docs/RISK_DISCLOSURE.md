# Risk Disclosure

XPERT Token and related smart contracts are experimental software. Blockchain transactions and smart-contract interactions carry technical, market, liquidity, custody, governance, regulatory, and operational risks.

## Principal Risks

- You may lose some or all of the capital you deposit.
- Smart contracts may contain bugs, vulnerabilities, accounting errors, or access-control failures.
- Token prices and liquidity may change rapidly or become unavailable.
- Transactions may fail, be delayed, or be irreversible.
- Governance actions may change fees, limits, permissions, or protocol behavior.
- Oracles, bridges, RPC providers, front ends, and external tokens may fail or be compromised.
- Audits, tests, or verification do not eliminate risk.
- Testnet status, planned features, and roadmap statements are not evidence of mainnet availability.

## User Responsibilities

Before interacting:

1. Verify the network and contract address from an authoritative source.
2. Review the source code, deployment status, fees, permissions, and security disclosures.
3. Use only capital you can afford to lose.
4. Protect private keys and verify transaction details before signing.
5. Do not rely on social posts, screenshots, or third-party claims as proof of returns or safety.

No contributor or maintainer guarantees profits, token appreciation, liquidity, security, or future availability.

**Trading and smart-contract activity involves capital loss. Past performance does not guarantee future returns.**
