## Proposed Fees

| Fee | Amount | Recipient | Frequency |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Entry | 0% | None | On deposit |
| Performance | 20% | Founder Vault | Monthly, subject to implementation |
| Management | 2% | Operations | Annual, subject to implementation |
| Withdrawal | 0% | None | On withdrawal |

Users must verify the effective fee parameters on-chain before depositing. Fees, returns, token value, and future allocations are not guaranteed.

## Reporting Standard

Public reports should distinguish realized results from projections and show methodology, time period, fees, drawdown, and relevant risks. Avoid dollar-profit screenshots or language implying guaranteed returns.

**Past performance does not guarantee future returns.**
